font-size: calc(var(--body) * pow(var(--base), STEP)); /* at least 0.75rem below the body */

**Arrangements.** A design chooses one of three, which fix each item's group and order. An item that is off leaves no gap, and the identity is always there.

- **A:** start: identity; end: search icon, globe, link menu, menu button, CTA.
- **B:** start: identity, link menu; end: search icon, globe, menu button, CTA.
- **C:** start: identity, link menu, search icon; end: globe, menu button, CTA.

**Items.**

- **Identity:** the brand, with an optional slogan, as one link to the start page. The logo is 2.25rem tall at its natural width. The name is in the heading typeface at the h5 size and letter spacing, in the emphasis weight, line height 1.15. Logo and name stand side by side, 0.625rem apart, or stacked from one start edge, 0.5rem apart. The slogan is in the small size, line height 1.3, `text-muted`: beside the brand it follows a 1 px hairline in `border`, one line tall, with 0.875rem on either side; below, it stands 0.5rem under the name, from the name's start edge, and with logo and name side by side the logo stands beside both lines, centred on them. Optical centring: give a line of words a box from its baseline to its cap height, and centre that. `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic` does it, but not in every browser (not in Firefox); what works everywhere is to make the line a flex row aligned on the baseline, with an empty `::before` one `1cap` tall, and the words in an inner element with a margin of `-0.5lh` above and below.
- **Link menu:** level 1 of the main menu as a row of links, the component gap apart, in the small size and letter spacing and the emphasis weight, in `text` with no underline; a 1 px underline 0.25em below the baseline on hover and on the current page.
- **Menu button:** the word "Menu" and a chevron that turns 180° while the megamenu is open, in the link menu's type, with no box; on hover the word is underlined. Or a hamburger icon in an icon control.
- **Globe:** the language switcher is always a globe icon, never a language's name, however many languages there are. It opens the language list.
- **Search icon:** a magnifier; it opens the search box.
- **CTA:** a filled button (see Button and Link Group).
- **Icon controls** (globe, magnifier, hamburger, close): 2.5rem square, the icon 1.25rem, transparent, control radius, in `text`. On hover a 1 px inset ring in `border` appears, and no fill.
- **Skip link:** "Skip to content" comes first, hidden until it has keyboard focus, then shown at the top start on the header's background with 0.5rem 0.75rem padding and control radius.

**Giving way.** When the bar would not fit on one line, it takes these steps in order until it fits, and takes them back as the window widens. A step that frees no width is skipped, and a slogan never outlasts the name.

1. the slogan goes
2. the name goes, where a logo stands beside it
3. the link menu goes, and a menu button appears if the bar had none
4. the menu button's word goes, and its chevron becomes a hamburger
5. the globe moves into the megamenu
6. the CTA moves into the megamenu
7. the search icon moves into the megamenu, as a search field

Moved items stand at the top of the megamenu, in the bar's order, in a wrapping row with 0.75rem 1.5rem gaps and a hairline in `border` 1.25rem below: the search field with its button, the globe followed by every language inline, and the CTA as a button. Nothing with a function is lost. A logo is at most 14rem wide, keeping its shape, so even a 20rem (320 px) window has room for it beside the menu button. If even the identity and the menu button do not fit, the identity scales down one step, to 0.8 of its size, and if that is not enough, a logo shrinks within its box and the name wraps to two balanced lines, centred in the bar, so the menu button always shows. The name is never cut.

How it is measured: write the bar once, with the steps it can take in order, and let a small script try them one after another in a single task, before the browser paints, so nothing flickers: it sets a state on the header naming the step, measures the groups and the gaps between them, and keeps the first step at which they are no wider than the bar's room, compared exactly, never letting the bar overflow; if none fits, it takes the last resort. Write it inline, right after the header, so it runs while the page is still being parsed and the right step is there before the first paint; loaded as a file it runs too late, and a wide page first shows its narrowest step. A ResizeObserver on the bar and its groups and the window's resize event, which a change of text size also fires, run it again. Without the script, show the narrowest step: the identity and the menu button, with everything else in the megamenu. A step whose item a page has taken away takes nothing. Do not write a hidden copy of the bar to measure by CSS: it doubles the markup, and search engines read its links twice.

**Shadow and line.** A sticky header casts the `header` shadow only once page content has scrolled under it, never at the top of the page, and the `header-panel` shadow while a panel is open. Where it casts none and the first band has the header's colour, at the top of the page too, a 1 px hairline in `border` parts them. In dark mode, where a shadow does not show on the dark page, the hairline stands beside the shadow too, wherever the header casts one. While a sticky header shows, the page's scroll padding at the top equals its height, so no anchor or focused element hides under it.

**Scrolling, without a script.** `position: sticky` holds the header. Its look follows three flags on the root element, each a discrete scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: scroll(root)`) of a custom property from 0 to 1 over a range twice as long as where it turns: content under the header (from 1 px), past T (1.5rem) and past the header's own height. Container style queries read them: the header is transparent with the first band's Surface roles while it is under the page's start and short of T, unless its megamenu is open (`:has()` of the megamenu's `:modal`), when it takes its own Surface as past T, and casts its shadow once content is under it. Sliding away reads the root scroller's last scroll direction with a scroll-state container query (`scroll-state(scrolled: block-end)`), together with the third flag, and keeps the header while a panel is open or the keyboard is in it (`:has(:modal, :popover-open, :focus-visible)`). Where a browser lacks scroll-driven animations (Firefox as of 2026-11), a small polyfill sets the same three flags on the root as the page scrolls; where it lacks scroll-state queries (Firefox and WebKit), another writes the last relative scroll direction on the root as an attribute that parallel selectors read. Each loads only where `CSS.supports` says the feature is missing, the second only for a header that slides away, and both set only what the CSS reads. Without them the header is opaque with its shadow from the start, and does not slide away.

**Megamenu.** The menu button opens a panel under the bar, a modal `<dialog>` the button opens with no script (`commandfor` and `command="show-modal"`, `closedby="any"`), placed absolutely in the top layer by the bar as its anchor (`anchor()`), its background edge to edge on the header's Surface and its content in the content area, with a 1 px hairline in `border` at its top and the `panel` shadow, and in dark mode a hairline at its bottom as well, since the shadow does not show there. Its rows stand 2rem apart, with 2rem above and 2.5rem below. Each level-1 item is a column, 2rem 1.5rem apart, headed by a link to its hub page in the h6 style, optionally numbered "01 /" in the label typeface in `link`, with 0.875rem below it. Its level-2 links stand under it, 0.875rem apart, in the body style in `text`, with no underline until hovered; each may have a description under it at 0.75 times the body size, in `text-muted`. In a window of 60rem or less the columns are two, and at 37.5rem or less one. An optional row above holds three columns of content, and an optional row below holds small links in `text-muted` under a hairline, 1.25rem above them; both go once the columns fall to two. The panel comes in with a fade and a 0.625rem slide down. It locks the page's scroll (`overflow: hidden` on the root while it is open) and scrolls inside when taller than the window; Esc or a click outside, the bar included, closes it, focus stays inside it while it is open and returns to the button. Only one panel is open at a time.

**Language list.** The globe opens a small list under it, a native popover (`popovertarget`), placed absolutely in the top layer by the globe as its anchor, 0.625rem below it, its end edge on the globe's end edge (`position-area: block-end span-inline-start`). It stands on the `page` Surface, at least 9.5rem wide, with 0.375rem padding, a 1 px `border`, control radius and the `panel` shadow. Each language stands in its own name, with 0.4375rem 0.625rem padding, the current one in the emphasis weight, and leads to the same page in that language.

**Search box.** The magnifier opens it under the header. The whole window, the header and the footer included, is covered by the `page` background at 62% opacity, over the page blurred by 0.75rem when the design says so: the blur is the dialog's `::backdrop` (`backdrop-filter`), and the fade a layer fixed to the window inside the dialog, which clicks pass through; both come in with a fade while the box slides down 0.625rem, and the dialog itself does not move, since a moved dialog would hold its fixed layer within itself. On it, 1.5rem below the header (0.75rem in a window of 37.5rem or less), a box is centred, as wide as the content area allows up to 36rem, on the Surface the design chose: 1.5rem padding (1.25rem in a window of 37.5rem or less), card radius, a 1 px `border` and the `panel` shadow, its parts 0.875rem apart. It holds an optional "Search" heading in the h5 style; a row with the field, which takes what the button leaves, and a filled Search button 0.625rem after it; and optional starting links in small type, in `link`, underlined, 0.375rem 1.125rem apart. In a window of 22.5rem or less the button goes under the field at full width. The field stands on the `page` Surface: `border-strong` 1 px, control radius, 0.625rem 0.75rem padding. Empty, it shows what the design chose: nothing, the site's own word for Search as its placeholder (in `text-muted`), or a magnifier at the icon size in `text-muted`, centred on the field's height where the words begin, which goes as soon as the visitor types, as a placeholder does (give the field a placeholder of one space, and hide the icon while `:placeholder-shown` does not match). A close icon control sits 0.75rem from the box's top end corner where the design has one; with no heading, the box keeps 3.75rem above the field for it. It is a modal `<dialog>` like the megamenu: focus moves to the field (`autofocus`), Esc closes the box, a click on the header lands on the backdrop and closes it by the dialog's light dismiss (`closedby="any"`), and a click beside the box lands on a transparent close button behind it, so a pointer closes it with no close button too; focus returns to the magnifier.

**A hint of the page** (where the design makes the header translucent). Where the bar is not clear, its background is its Surface's at an opacity of its own, and so is the megamenu's and the open language list's, each over the page blurred by 1rem (`backdrop-filter: blur(1rem)`), so what lies under it shows only as a hint. The opacity is the least at which every role the layer draws on its background keeps its contrast over any page, from black to white: `text`, `text-muted` and `link` at 4.5:1, and `border-strong`, `focus` and `button-background` at 3:1, the background laid over the page as CSS lays a colour of that opacity over another; and never under 85%, so the page is only sensed. For the `page` Surface that is 88% in light mode and 85% in dark mode, for `brand` 95%. Clear over the page's start, the bar stays clear, with no blur. In print the bar is opaque.

**Motion.** The header's colours change over the feedback duration, and its slide away and back over the movement duration (see Shared rules).

### Footer

**Type.** All of the footer is in the small size and line height. The lower band takes the label size only: its typeface, weight, case and letter spacing stay the footer's. Text is `text`, quieter text `text-muted` at most 30em wide, and a cell's heading takes the h5 style.

**Bands.** An upper band and a thin lower band, each on its own Surface, edge to edge, with its content in the content area. The upper band has the `band` space above and below and `band-run` between its rows; the lower band has 1.25rem above and below and 0.5rem between its rows. Two bands on one Surface close up as any two Bands do: the upper loses its end space, and the lower starts with `band-run`.

**Cells.** Each band is a grid of two columns and two rows, cells A1, B1, A2 and B2. Column A takes a third of the content area less the column gap, `(content area − column gap) / 3`, and column B the rest, after the column gap. A row is as tall as it needs, and an empty row or column takes no room. One filled cell spans all four; two in one column each span their row; two in one row keep their columns; for the rest, the design's fill rule says which neighbour an empty cell gives way to. A cell's content box is as small as its content allows, at the top of the cell, against its start edge in column A and its end edge in column B, whatever the cell has come to cover; its text is always start-aligned, and its parts stand in a column the component gap apart.

**Content.** What stands in the cells is the site's own: typically the brand, social icons, a short text and links in the upper band, and address, registration and legal links in the lower. Links stand inline, parted by a • in `text-muted` with 0.7em on either side, and no line starts with a •. A link is `text`, underlined 0.22em below the baseline only on hover and on the current page. Icon links stand in a wrapping row 1rem apart, each icon 1.75rem. The brand is the header's (logo, name, as the header shows them, without the slogan), its logo at most 14rem wide; on a Surface dark in both modes use the dark logo, elsewhere the one for the mode.

**Lines.** Hairlines in `border`, only where the design asks for them: one between the rows, one between the columns. A line never parts two colours or crosses a merged cell, and stops at the content unless the design says otherwise.

**On a phone.** Where the footer is 37.5rem wide or less, each band is one column with the side margin as its inline padding: the upper band column by column (A1, A2, B1, B2) and the lower band row by row (A1, B1, A2, B2), every box at the start edge, and no lines. Write each band's cells in that order in the document, so reading and stacking orders agree, and place them on the wide grid by their own column and row.

**Attachment.** At the end of the page, it scrolls in after the last Band. At the edge of the screen, it lies still at the window's bottom under the page, which slides off it as the reader reaches the end. This holds only while the whole footer fits in the window, a footer exactly as tall included; a footer taller than the window is at the end of the page, in the flow. A small script, loaded only by a footer at the edge of the screen, compares the footer's height with the window's, with a ResizeObserver on the footer and the window's resize event, which a change of text size also fires, and sets one state on the footer saying whether it fits. While it fits, the footer is `position: sticky` at the window's bottom edge (`inset-block-end: 0`), under the page's main, which is opaque, `position: relative` and above it. Without the script the footer simply stands at the end of the page. While keyboard focus is in the footer (`:has(:focus-visible)`) it is `position: relative`, where the browser shows what has focus.

**Where page and footer meet.** The page lies over the footer, so any shadow falls from the page's bottom edge onto the footer, under either attachment. Where the design gives the page its shadow, the `page` shadow always shows there. Where it does not, and the footer is at the edge of the screen, the page casts the `page` shadow anyway when its last colour (its last Band's Surface, or the page Surface where the last child has none) is the same as the footer's first band, since its edge must show; one rule per Surface kind compares the two in CSS. Wherever the two colours are the same and no shadow results (the design has no shadows, or the page's shadow is off and the footer is at the end of the page), a 1 px hairline in `border` parts them; a design may also ask for that line always. In dark mode the hairline also stands wherever the page casts its shadow on the footer, since a shadow does not show on dark colours. Different colours need nothing more.

### Hero

**What a hero can be.** The design sets the default and a page may depart from it: the picture none, behind the text under an overlay, or beside it at the inline start or end, inside the content width or bleeding to the window's edge; for no picture or one beside, the background none or a Surface; for a picture behind, a dark or a light overlay; a height of a third, half, two thirds or all of the screen; the text at the top, in the middle or at the bottom, and, with no picture beside it, at the start, centre or end of the content width; the heading in the h1 or the display style; and the page's H1 in the hero or first in the next section. This design's choices are under This design's frame.

**The band.** A hero is a Band with the `band` space above and below its text, and it keeps its end space even where the next Band has its Surface, so the middle is the true middle. Its height is at least its share of what the header leaves of the window, `100svh` less the header's height, or of all `100svh` when the page starts under the header; taller content makes it taller.

**The text.** From the top: the trail, when the H1 is here; a logo, where a picture stands before the text, at its own proportions, at most 16rem wide and 4rem tall, the column gap below it; the eyebrow in the label style, `text-muted`; the heading in the chosen style, and an article's subheadline under it; the deck in the lead style, `text-muted`; then the actions (Actions), the column gap below the deck, with fine print under them as their note, the component gap apart. The last picture a hero holds is its picture; where the design has no picture but the hero holds one, it stands beside the text at the end. Heading, subheadline and deck are at most the Narrow measure wide, a display heading at most 14em. With no picture beside it, the text block is as wide as its words and moves across the content width; its lines stay start-aligned.

**A picture behind.** It covers the hero, centred, on the `picture-dark` Surface (or `picture-light` for a light overlay), whose roles colour everything on it, such as `picture-dark-text`, `picture-dark-text-muted`, `picture-dark-link`, `picture-dark-border-strong` and `picture-dark-button-background`. The overlay is one layer of #000000 at 0.57 opacity (light: #ffffff at 0.63 opacity), the strength at which the text on it passes its contrast check over any picture, from black to white. So wherever text stands the layer has exactly that strength, and nowhere is it stronger: where fades overlap they never add up past it, so the picture is never covered more than the check needs. With the text at the bottom, the layer has its full strength from the hero's bottom edge up to the top edge of the text block. Above that it fades out over `clamp(6rem, 24svh, 16rem)`: going up from the text's top edge it has 84% of its strength a quarter of the way, 50% halfway, 16% at three quarters and nothing at the end, and the picture is clear above. With the text at the top it is the mirror: full strength from the hero's top edge down to the text's last line, then the same fade going down. In the middle the layer lies evenly over the whole picture. Under a transparent header the header's words keep the full strength behind them.

**A picture beside.** The content area is split into two equal halves with the column gap between them; the text stands in one half at the chosen place, and the picture fills the other half's height, cropped to cover, with the card radius. A bleeding picture runs to the window's edge on its side and through the hero's top and bottom space, with no radius. In a window of 40rem or less the picture stands above the text, at least 16:9 of its own width, with the column gap below it, and the hero grows when the two need more than its share.

### The frame

- **Page:** The whole page, with the header above and the footer below. Its main content is a vertical sequence of Bands, nothing else. The page's own Surface, `page`, runs the window's full width. Header, page and footer together fill at least the window's height: on a short page the page grows, in its own colour below the last Band, so the footer stands at the window's bottom. It is always opaque, and its background scrolls with it.
- **Header:** The site header: identity, navigation, a menu button with a responsive megamenu, and a search icon that opens a search panel. Arrangements A, B and C; sticky or static; its items give way as the window narrows. Its recipe is under Header above.
- **Footer:** Two bands, an upper one and a thin lower one, each with up to four cells: a logo, links, text, icons, legal lines. It sits at the end of the page or lies at the edge of the screen, revealed as the page scrolls. Its recipe is under Footer above.
- **Band:** One section of a page. It runs edge to edge, owns its vertical space, may have a Surface of its own, and keeps its content inside the content area. It has the `band` space above and below its content, which lies in the content area. Two adjacent Bands on the same Surface, or both on none, close up: the first loses its end space and the second starts with `band-run`. A Band may have a picture as its background, cropped to cover and centred.
- **Reading Area:** Long text in the Narrow measure, optionally with an Aside for a table of contents, sidenotes and the like. It only lays out across the width: no background and no vertical space of its own, always inside a Band. Where the Narrow column stands is under Layout. **Aside Block:** content for the whole text, such as a table of contents or a contact box, at the top of the Aside, the component gap between blocks; unplated it has a 1 px `border` hairline at its top and the component gap below that, except one that begins with a table of contents, which has neither, so the menu's label stands level with the text; on a Surface plate padding and the card radius; it may stick under the header, at most as tall as the window below it and scrolling inside itself where taller, where nothing in the text reaches into the Aside. **Sidenote:** a note anchored to a paragraph, in small type and `text-muted`, with a 1 px `border-strong` rule at its inline start and the component gap inside it, its heading in `text`; in two columns it stands in the Aside from the end of the paragraph it follows, below the Aside Blocks and the note before, and in one column it follows its paragraph. **Breakout:** an element of the text that is `wide`, `full`, `bleed-start` or `bleed-end` (Layout); where an Aside Block or a note stands beside it, it keeps Narrow, and the text goes on in Narrow below it. **Two columns** start at a window width worked out from Narrow, the column gap, the Aside's minimum and the side margin, in rem, so they follow the reader's text size; no script measures anything.

### Basics

- **Body Text:** Headings, paragraphs, lists and links, wherever they stand. The body style in `text`, wrapping anywhere a long word needs. Paragraphs, lists and rules have 1em below them, and the first and last child no outer margin. Lists are indented 1.5em, their items 0.35em apart; bullets are in `accent` and numbers in `text-muted` with tabular digits (List markers has the rest). Headings take their level's style, with 1.6em above and 0.6em below, their lines balanced; h2 to h6 in the heading typeface, h1 in the first heading's. Links are `link`, underlined 0.06em thick and 0.18em below the baseline, 0.12em thick on hover. Strong text takes the emphasis weight, emphasis the typeface's own italic. A quotation looks as Quotation does, so it cannot be taken for the text round it: its words in the h4 style at the body weight, in `text`, after a 0.125rem rule in `accent` with the body size between, and 1.5 times the body size above and below it. Its source, a `cite` alone in its last paragraph, stands 0.75 times the body size under the words, in small type, upright, in `text-muted`. In a card, a fact box, a sidenote or an Aside Block a quotation keeps their size. A rule is a 1 px `border` line with 2em above and below. Inline code is the code typeface at 0.9em; superscripts and subscripts are 0.75em and leave the line height alone.
- **Heading Group:** An eyebrow, a heading and a deck, at any heading level. Eyebrow in the label style, heading in the chosen style (h2 by default, its level free), deck in the lead style; eyebrow and deck in `text-muted`, the heading in `text` with balanced lines. They stand half the component gap apart, but an eyebrow a quarter of it from the heading it labels, closer than the heading stands to its deck. Standing directly in a Band with more after it, the group has the column gap below it. **Subheadline:** an article's headline may have a third part under the main headline, in the h4 style, balanced. A deck may hold a link, in `link`, underlined 0.06em thick (0.12em on hover), and words in bold, in the emphasis weight and `text`.
- **Button and Link Group:** A filled button, an unfilled button and an arrow link, alone or several in a row. **Filled:** `button-background` with `button-text`, `button-background-hover` on hover; the main typeface at the small size and letter spacing, in the emphasis weight, line height 1.2; padding 0.8em 1.3em, no border, the control radius; an icon goes 0.5em from the words, and words too long for the line wrap, centred, rather than widen what holds the button, a word longer than the line breaking too. Where the design has a frame, it is an inset ring, in the colour Shapes gives for a filled button. **Unfilled:** transparent, `text`, with a 1 px inset ring in `border-strong` (the frame's width if wider), which turns `text` on hover. **Arrow link:** no box and no padding, `link`, the words followed by an arrow that moves 0.2em forward on hover; at least 1.5rem square as a target. Colours change over the feedback duration. **Link Group:** buttons and links in a row that wraps, the component gap apart, or stacked from the start edge.
- **Icon:** A one-colour icon that follows the text colour. A line icon 1em square, stroked in the current text colour at width 2 on a 24-unit grid with round caps and joins and no fill, set 0.125em below the baseline. Which icon goes where, and where to get it, is under Icons above.
- **Actions:** What the reader can do next, each a link: a main action and others, or links to social media. A row that wraps, the component gap apart. The main action, the one the editor set in bold (at most one), is a filled button and the others unfilled buttons (Button and Link Group), each at least 2.75rem tall. A link to a known service, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or X, is drawn as its icon (Icons) at 1.2em in a box at least 2.75rem square, its words kept for a screen reader only. Where every action is such a link they stand as a row of bare icons 0.25rem apart, each 1.5em with 0.5rem around it, in `text`, `link` on hover, with no box; a main one among them stays filled. A lone main action right after a series shows more of it: centred under the series, the column gap above, drawn as a link in the body size and `link`, with no box, underlined on hover, followed by a chevron-shaped arrow that moves 0.2em forward on hover. In a hero, a section's head and a card the actions take the place the part gives them; in a card at its foot, so cards in a row end alike.

### Bands

- **Hero:** The page's opening, taking a third, half, two thirds or all of the screen. A picture can lie behind the text, under an overlay that follows the text, or stand on one side with the text opposite, inside the content width or bleeding to the window's edge and the hero's top and bottom. The text stands at the top, in the middle or at the bottom, and with no picture at the side it moves across the content width, its lines start-aligned. Its background is free, none or a coloured Surface, and so is its heading, in the first heading or the display style, which is only a look. The page's H1 is its heading, or stands under it in the next section, and then its text is a plain paragraph with the same look. Its recipe is under Hero above.
- **Text and Image:** Text on one side and an image on the other, either way round. Two cells in a row, each at least 24rem before they wrap, the column gap apart, centred on each other; the image at the end by default or at the start, with the card radius. When they wrap, the text comes first.
- **CTA Band:** A heading, a short text and a button, often last on the page. A Band on the `tint` Surface by default. A Heading Group with its heading in the h3 style, a short text in `text-muted` at most Narrow wide with the component gap above it, and the buttons at the end side, the column gap apart; the buttons wrap under the text when the row is narrow (the text wants 28rem).
- **Key Figures:** A few large numbers with explanations. A grid of four columns, or two, or one, never three and one, each item at least 12rem, the column gap apart. Each item has a 1 px `border` hairline at its top with the component gap below it, then the value in the h1 style in `text` with tabular digits, then its label and an optional note in small type, `text-muted`, half the component gap apart.
- **Section:** One band of a page made of up to three groups, media, a head and a body, laid out by what they hold. A Band of up to three groups, in this order, each left out when empty: **media**, the picture the section begins with and any pictures right after it as its badges; **the head**, any eyebrows and the h2 after them, its deck (the paragraph right after the heading) and any actions right after; and **the body**, everything else, in the order written. Without that h2 there is no head. A paragraph is no deck where only paragraphs follow the heading, or where the section holds a form or a choice. A deck is long where it holds more than about 70 characters, a line beside its heading. The head's parts stand the component gap apart, its heading and deck at most Narrow wide. In the body, running text keeps the Narrow measure and a part with a look of its own stands the column gap from its neighbours. The layout follows from what the groups hold, by six rules, the first that matches deciding, and with none the head stands above the body. Below a rule's window width everything stands in one column, in document order, the column gap apart.
  1. **A picture first:** from a 48rem window, two equal columns, the picture in the first, full width with the card radius and its badges after it (the component gap apart, each at most 8rem wide and 4rem tall), and the head and body in the second, centred on the picture's height, the column gap between them. Where the head and body are taller, the picture grows to their height, cropped to fill it (`object-fit: cover`), never short of its own proportions, and at most `min(100cqi, 85svh)` tall, where `cqi` is the picture's column, so a square at the most; beside text longer still it keeps that size and is sticky at the top of the window, as a following menu is. With badges, the picture and its badges stay together at their own size and are sticky together. With a form in the body the picture is what the form offers: whole, not cropped, without radius, centred, at most 26rem wide (16rem stacked) and `min(36rem, 70svh)` tall.
  2. **A heading with a form right after it**, the head holding neither deck nor actions: from 48rem, the head and the body side by side at 2:3, the form beside the heading, any fine print under the form.
  3. **Another form, or a choice:** the whole section centred, at most Narrow wide, its text centred, while a form and a choice's panels keep their text start-aligned; it reads as a page's end.
  4. **A head and an accordion alone:** from 60rem, the head beside the accordion at 2:3, and the head stays in view under the header as the page scrolls (sticky, the component gap below the header), at most as tall as the window below the header less the component gap, scrolling inside itself where it is taller, so its end is never out of reach; no script.
  5. **A head and paragraphs only:** from 60rem, the paragraphs beside the head at 2:3.
  6. **A head and a series** (cards, steps, reasons, cards that lead on or posts; the first in the body decides): the head is centred over reasons, over cards with neither picture nor symbol, and over exactly two cards; where the deck is long and the series has pictures and five cards or more, from 60rem the heading and the deck stand side by side in two equal columns, the deck level with the heading's last line; otherwise the head stands above, at the start.
- **Logos:** A band of logos under their label. The label in the label style and `text-muted`, centred, the component gap above the logos. The logos stand in rows from the middle, the component gap between rows and the column gap between logos, each as tall as `clamp(2.5rem, 1.9286rem + 2.8571vw, 4.5rem)`, 2.5rem in the narrowest window and 4.5rem from 90rem, at its own proportions, at most four times as wide as tall, shown whole. A logo that is a link keeps its picture and takes the focus ring with the control radius. Alone at the top of a page it stands in a section of its own, and such a band of logos alone stands as far from the band before it as from the band after it: where the band beside it has its colour, the band space parts them, not the smaller space of a run, and a Hero before it keeps its own end space in place of the logos' start.
- **Article:** An article's page: an opening, the body with its following menu, and Read more. **The opening** is a Hero whose headline has up to three parts: an eyebrow (or, without one, the category) above the main headline, and a subheadline under it (Heading Group); the standfirst follows as the deck, then a meta line in small type and `text-muted`: the site's sentence of the byline, the published date and the category, such as "By A. Writer • Published 10 October 2026 in Guides", each author and the category a link to its page, underlined in the line's colour and `text` on hover; then a • and the article's topics as pills, each a link to its topic's page, 0.4em apart, underlined on hover. The • stays with the sentence, so the line never starts with one. **The body** is a Band holding a Reading Area with the following menu "In this article" in the Aside, before the text (Table of Contents), and the text as Body Text: a lede, then crossheads, h2, in the h3 style, and the headings under them a step lower each (h3 in the h4 style, h4 in the h5, h5 and h6 in the h6). A paragraph wholly in bold is an interview's question, with 1.6em above it and 0.5em below, so it stays with its answer. Fact boxes stand in the text, and the menu's stops are every crosshead and every fact box with an anchor. **Read more** is a Section of up to three teaser cards under a heading, with a lone main action to the rest (Actions).

### Cards

- **Card:** A picture or icon, heading, text and link. Variants: link and person. It can sit on any Surface. A column, its parts the component gap apart, as tall as its grid row. A card has no Surface unless it is given one, so cards in a Card Grid on a plain Band are hairline cards. **Without a Surface** it has a 1 px `border` hairline at its top with the component gap below it, and no box; a picture on top replaces the hairline. **On a Surface** it has plate padding, the card radius, the design's frame and the `box` shadow, and a picture on top runs flush to its top and sides, clipped to the radius. A picture is 16:9 (a person's 1:1), cropped to cover, with the card radius. An icon stands at the h2 size in `text`. Eyebrow in the label style, `text-muted`, a quarter of the component gap above the heading; heading in the h5 style, balanced; text in small type, `text-muted`. **Link variant:** the whole card is the link, through its heading, with an arrow in `link` at its foot; on hover or focus the heading turns `link` and underlined, a card on a Surface lifts to the `box-lifted` shadow and, where the design has a `box` shadow, rises 0.125rem with it over the feedback duration (not where the visitor asks for reduced motion), and focus rings the whole card. The arrow stays still, as a word such as "Read more" would. The background never changes. Every card that leads somewhere behaves so, a Content Card too. **In body text** a card keeps its own look, its link included. **Person variant:** a square portrait, name, role in small type, and email and phone as ordinary links in `link`, each with an icon 0.5em before it.
- **Content Card and Content Row:** Article teasers with picture, metadata, heading, text and an optional separate call to action, as cards or rows. Both stack: metadata, heading, metadata, short text, call to action, 0.6em apart. **Content Card** has the Card's geometry: no Surface means no box, and a picture on top in place of the hairline; the picture is 3:2 (or 2:1), cropped, with the card radius, on `background-alt` while it loads, and on a Surface it is flush or inset in the plate. **Content Row:** the picture in a column `clamp(5rem, 22%, 14rem)` wide at the start, the text beside it, the component gap apart and above and below, a 1 px `border` hairline between rows; in 24rem or less the picture goes on top. **Metadata** (such as the date and category) is in the label style, `text-muted`, its items parted by a dot (or a comma or bar), never at a line's start. **Heading:** h5 style in `text`, a link that turns `link` and underlined when the item is hovered or focused, and a plated card lifts to `box-lifted` and rises as a Card does; a row stays still. The call to action stays still too. **Text:** small, `text-muted`. **Call to action:** a link in small type, the emphasis weight and `link`, optionally ending in ›, », → or ➔, or an unfilled button. **Pill:** a category as a pill in the label style, capitals, padding 0.15em 0.6em, line height 1.2, radius 2em, `button-background` with `button-text`. A group of rows (such as a year) has a small `text-muted` label followed by a hairline. A search hit is marked on `background-alt`. **In body text** a loop keeps its own look, its links included: a title is not drawn as a body link.
- **Card Grid:** Lays out cards in two to four columns that break on narrow screens. Columns of equal width, three by default (one to four), each at least 15rem, the column gap between them in both directions; fewer columns where they do not fit (four go to two, never to three and one), and every card as tall as its row. This is for a grid whose columns are set; a series of cards takes its columns from its number of cards (Card series).
- **Card series:** Cards, in a series whose number of cards sets its columns: open, links as a whole, actions, numbers, pictures behind, symbols and signs. **Columns:** two for two cards, three for one or three, and from four on three or four, whichever leaves the last row fuller, four where both do alike, so three where the count is 5, 6 or 9 more than a multiple of 12 and four otherwise. Only the cards that show count. Each column is at least 15rem; where four do not fit, the same choice is made between three and two, so four cards fall to two and two, never three and one, and where three do not fit, two, then one. The columns fill the row even with fewer cards, so a lone card keeps its width. The column gap between cards in both directions (the component gap in an accordion), and in body text the column gap above and below the series. **Open:** a card is open, a Card without a Surface, with its top hairline, unless it is a link as a whole. **A link as a whole**, a card whose heading holds its only link: on the `raised` Surface (`page` where the band is `raised`), lifting on hover as the link variant does. **An action card**, a card with actions in a series where some card has none: on `tint` (`raised` where the band is `tint`). A list in a card takes its small, muted type, and its actions stand at its foot so that cards in a row end alike. **A number or an address** alone in its paragraph is a link in the h6 style in `link`, underlined, a phone's digits tabular, on one line where it fits; "Call us" stays an ordinary link. **Leading on:** cards under a `text-muted` label, the component gap apart, each a link card with its arrow. **Pictures behind:** each card with a picture stands on `picture-dark`, at least 20rem tall, its text at its foot, the picture filling the card, cropped to cover, under that Surface's scrim; a card without a picture is an ordinary card. **Symbols:** a card's picture as a symbol, shown whole in a box the same on every card: by the card, twice as wide as high and 1.25 times the h2 size high, the symbol at its start; in the heading, a square of 1.25em before its words, 0.4em from them. An inline SVG symbol takes `accent`. **Signs:** in a ticked or crossed series the mark of Reasons hangs before each heading.
- **Timeline:** Steps in order, numbered as the editor writes them. The columns as for a Card series. Each step opens with its number as written, in any numbering and punctuation of List markers, on a filled button's plate: `button-background` with `button-text`, at least 2.4em square with 0.6em at the sides, the control radius, the heading typeface at the h6 size and weight, tabular digits. A 1 px `border-strong` line runs from the number, 0.75em after it, to the step's end, centred on it, leading the eye on. Then the heading in the h5 style, balanced, and the step's text in `text-muted`, the component gap apart.
- **Reasons:** Ticked or crossed points without pictures. The columns as for a Card series. Each reason is a heading in the h5 style with its mark before it, then its text in `text-muted`, the component gap apart. The mark is 0.9em, 0.4em before the heading's words, drawn as a filled shape, no image: a check in `accent` or a cross in `text-muted`. It hangs before the heading, indented 1.3 times the h5 size, so the heading's lines and the text share one edge. In forced colours it takes the text colour.
- **Post List:** Teasers for a site's posts: as cards, the latest featured, or filtered by their categories, topics and a search. Each teaser is a card that is a link as a whole: its picture, its category as the eyebrow, its headline as the card's only link, and its text. **As cards:** a Card series. **Featured:** the latest first, its heading in the h3 style, then the next two; by the list's own width, one column, from 32rem two with the latest across both, and from 50rem three with the latest over two columns and two rows, its picture growing to match the two beside it; any further posts follow as the small ones. **Filtered:** Visitor Filters over the cards, in the rows chosen where the list is placed, always in this order: a row of the categories' pills, a row of the topics' (and of any other taxonomy's), each with the terms with the most posts first, and a search row last; a row the page itself settles, such as the category on a category's page, is left out. With no pill pressed every card shows; pressed pills show the cards with any of their row's terms, and pills on further rows narrow that to the cards with one of each row's too, at once, by CSS. A search goes to the site's search page with the pressed pills and what the list and the page settle, such as the posts' type and the category, so a search from a row's choice searches within it.

### Content

- **Table:** A header row, a caption, column alignment and alternating rows, with aligned digits. Full width, in the small style, `text`. Cells have 0.75em 0.9em padding, start-aligned and at the top, with a 1 px `border` line under each row; the head row's line is `border-strong`, as is the line above a footer row. Head cells take the emphasis weight. Numbers align at the end with tabular digits. The caption stands above, small, `text-muted`, start-aligned, 0.75em above the table. Stripes, where asked for, are `background-alt` on every second body row; a hovered row takes `background-alt` (`background-hover` when striped) over the feedback duration. A wide table scrolls sideways in its own box, 1.5em above and below. On a Surface: the component gap as padding and the card radius.
- **Accordion:** Headings that fold out. A FAQ is this component with questions and answers. Items parted by 1 px `border` hairlines, one above the first and one under each, 1.5em above and below the whole. A summary is a row at least 2.75rem tall, with 1em above and below and 0.75em at the sides: its label in the h6 style and a chevron at the end that turns 180° when open; on hover it takes `background-alt`, with no underline. The content has 0.75em at the sides and 1em below, and opens over the movement duration. **Boxes:** separate items 0.5em apart, each with a 1 px `border-strong` border and the control radius, the summary on `background-alt`, `background-hover` on hover. **Questions and answers:** an accordion whose headings are all questions (each ends in a question mark) is drawn as questions and answers: the question in the h5 style, a step above other accordions' labels, and the answer indented 1.75em at its start, with a 0.1875rem rule in `accent` 0.75em from its start edge, running from its top (in boxes, 1em below the summary) to 1em above its end. **One open at a time**, where asked for: give the items one shared `name`, so the browser closes the others; otherwise each opens on its own, and the first may start open. Cards inside an accordion stand the component gap apart, not the column gap.
- **Quotation:** A prominent quotation with name and title. The words in the h4 style, but in the body weight, in `text`, after a 0.125rem rule in `accent` at the inline start with 1em between; 1.5em above and below. The name and title follow in small type, `text-muted`, 0.75em below, the name in `text`, as "Name, title", and a source link after a ·. On a Surface: plate padding, the card radius and no rule.
- **Callout:** A marked box in the text: information, warning, success or error. **Filled** (the default): the box stands on the `status-info`, `status-success`, `status-warning` or `status-error` Surface, with a 0.3rem band in that Surface's `accent` at the inline start, 1.25em padding and the control radius, 1.5em above and below. A heading row holds the status icon (an i, a check or an x in a circle, or a mark in a triangle) in the band's colour and an optional title in the h6 style, 0.6em apart and 0.6em above the body text. **Outlined:** no fill, on the surrounding Surface, with a 1 px frame and the same 0.3rem start band in that Surface's `info`, `success`, `warning` or `error` role.
- **Fine Print:** A note in small type: under a hero's actions, or a form's terms after the form. Small type in `text-muted`, at most Narrow wide; its links underlined in its own colour, turning `text` on hover. Under a hero's actions it is their note, and after a form the form's terms, the component gap below what it belongs to.
- **Fact Box:** A box of facts in the reading column. In the flow of the text, not in the Aside, 1.5em above and below. On a Surface (`tint`, or `raised` where the band is `tint`): plate padding and the card radius, with neither frame nor shadow; without one, a 1 px `border` hairline at its top and the component gap below it. Its label in the label style and `text-muted`, 0.5em above its heading in the h5 style, balanced, 0.6em below it; its lists in small type, with the design's bullets. With an anchor it is a stop in an article's following menu.
- **List markers:** A list's signs and numberings, as the editor writes them. **Signs:** a check or a cross before each item in place of a bullet, drawn as strokes, no image or character: 0.8em, centred on the first line, hanging 1.4em before the text, the list indented 1.6em; the check two strokes 0.13em thick in `accent`, the cross two crossed strokes in `text-muted`. **Numberings:** decimal, lower or upper latin, lower or upper roman, lower greek and double latin (aa, bb, …, zz, aaa), each with a full stop after it, a closing parenthesis after it, brackets around it, or nothing. Latin and greek letters double after their last (y, z, aa, bb). Keep the list a real numbered list (with `@counter-style`), its markers in `text-muted` with tabular digits, and give it room for its widest marker: about 1.5em for a digit or a letter, 2em from ten items and 2.5em from a hundred, 2.25em for lower roman and 2.75em for upper, more with brackets. **Dotted**, below the first level (1.1, A.1): the number of the item above, a full stop and the item's own, drawn before the item where a marker would hang. A level without a marker of its own takes the nearest level above of its kind. **The design's own**, where no marker is set, in body text, cards and fact boxes alike: bullets in `accent`, numbers in `text-muted` with tabular digits. **A task list:** a checkbox, 0.9167rem, in place of each bullet, hanging 0.55em before the text and centred on the first line, with `accent-color` set to `accent`, drawn light.
- **Description List:** Terms beside their descriptions. Each term beside its descriptions, a third of the width for the term and two thirds for them, the column gap between, aligned on their first baselines; a 1 px `border` hairline above each pair, the component gap above and below its content (none below the last), the descriptions 0.5em apart. Where the list is narrower than 32rem the term stands above them. The term is in the h6 style and `text` but is no heading. A label in the label style may introduce the list.
- **Value List:** Values that stand out under their labels, such as opening hours and telephone numbers. Pairs side by side, as many as fit, each at least 14rem, the component gap between rows and the column gap between columns. Each pair stands under a 1 px `border` hairline, the component gap below it: its label in small type and `text-muted`, then, 0.25em below, the value in the h4 style in `text` with lining digits; a value that is a link, such as a telephone number, in `link`. Key Figures stays for a few large numbers.
- **Tabs:** Content in tabs, each tab's heading its button. An exclusive group of native disclosures (`<details name>`), the first open, drawn as tabs, with no script. An optional label in the label style and `text-muted`, 0.5em above, names the group. The tabs, each a disclosure's summary, stand in a row that wraps, 0.25em apart, over a 1 px `border` hairline at the top of the open panel; each is at least 2.75rem tall, with 0.55em 0.85em padding, in the h6 style and `text-muted`, with a transparent 0.1875rem bar at its foot and the control radius at its top corners. On hover it takes `text` on `background-alt`; the open tab is `text` with its bar in `accent`, so its state is a shape as well as a colour. The panel follows, the component gap below. A panel of text followed by other components stands in two columns where both fit, each at least 18rem, the component gap between rows and the column gap between columns; any other panel keeps its order in one column. Headings inside a panel are promoted a level, an h4 becoming an h3, never above h2. **Behaviour:** the browser's own. One tab is open and only its panel shows; a click, Enter or Space opens a tab and closes the others, and a click on the open tab keeps it open. Tab moves through the tabs, each followed by what its open panel holds; there are no arrow keys between tabs. A screen reader hears each tab as a disclosure that is expanded or collapsed. A link to something in a closed panel opens it. Where a browser cannot lay the disclosures out as tabs, they read as an accordion; in print every panel shows under its tab's name.
- **Choice:** A question whose answers are big buttons, each showing what follows from it. An optional label names the answers, the component gap above them. An exclusive group of native disclosures (`<details name>`), none open, with no script. The answers, each a disclosure's summary, stand in a row that wraps, each at least 14rem, the component gap apart. Each is drawn as a big button on `raised` (`page` where the band is `raised`), at least 4rem tall, with 1em 1.25em padding, the card radius, in the h5 style, start-aligned, with a 1 px inset ring in `border-strong` (the frame's width if wider) and the `box` shadow, and at its end a round mark 1.125em across with a 2 px `border-strong` ring, as a radio button draws it. On hover the ring and the mark turn `text` and the shadow lifts to `box-lifted`; chosen, the ring is 2 px in `accent` and the mark is ringed and filled with `accent`. The chosen answer's panel stands the component gap below the answers: where it holds more than a form, on a plate (`raised`, or `page` where the band is `raised`) with plate padding, the frame, the card radius and the `box` shadow; a panel of only a form stands on the band. Headings inside a panel are promoted a level, as in Tabs. **Behaviour:** the browser's own, in a group named by the label. None is chosen beforehand, and no panel shows until one is. A click, Enter or Space chooses an answer and shows its panel alone, under the answers; a click on the chosen one keeps it chosen; a link to something in a panel chooses its answer. Where a browser cannot lay the disclosures out side by side, each panel stands under its own answer; in print every panel shows under its answer. A section holding a choice is centred (Section, rule 3).

### Forms and consent

- **Form:** Fields, text areas, selects, checkboxes, radio buttons and error messages: enough for contact and subscription. Fields in one column, or two of at least 16rem each, the component gap apart. A field is its label in `text` in the body weight (and "(required)" in `text-muted`), then the control, then help, 0.4em apart. Checkboxes and radio buttons are 1.1em, with `accent-color` set to `accent`, drawn light on every Surface, each on a row at least 2.75rem tall, 0.6em from its words. Help text is small and `text-muted`. An error turns the control's border `error` and shows a message in small type in `error`, with a circle-x icon 0.5em before it. A disabled control is on `background-alt` with a `border` border and `text-muted` words. The submit buttons stand in a Link Group.
- **Consent Banner:** The consent box and its settings. **Banner:** a card at the bottom end corner, 1em from the edges, up to 37rem wide, 1.5em padding, on the `page` Surface, with a 1 px `border`, the card radius and the `panel` shadow; in a window of 40rem or less, full width at the bottom with no lower radius. A heading in the h5 style, a short text in small type on purposes and withdrawal, a policy link, then Accept all and Reject optional as two identical filled buttons at full width, 0.6em apart, and Customise choices as an unfilled button. **Settings:** a dialog up to 54rem wide, 1.5em padding, a 1 px `border`, the card radius and the `panel` shadow over the `page` background at 60% opacity, with the title in the h4 style and a close control 2.75rem square. A switch is a track 2.6em by 1.5em with radius 2em, a 1 px `border-strong` border on the Surface's `background`, and a round knob 1.1em across in `text`; switched on, the track takes the button colours and the knob slides 1em along. A necessary purpose is on, disabled and marked "Always on". Accept and reject stay in view at the bottom, filled, at the start, and Save choices, unfilled, at the end. **After a choice:** a round 2.75rem button with a cookie icon stays at the bottom start corner, with a 1 px `border` and the `panel` shadow, to reopen the settings, and the footer leaves room for it at its end.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do build every page as a header, Bands and a footer, and put each Band on a Surface.
- Do take every colour on a Surface from that same Surface, and check the page in every mode the site uses.
- Do let adjacent Bands differ in Surface to give a page rhythm; use the strong Surfaces, `brand`, `inverse` and `accent`, sparingly.
- Do use one first heading per page, and keep running text in the Narrow measure.
- Do keep text on the type scale, and use the fluid sizes rather than breakpoints of your own.
- Do work at both a phone's width and a wide screen, since the narrow and wide type scales differ.
- Do use the logo as given, in its own colours, and the name and slogan as written.
- Don't use a display style for a second heading or smaller.
- Don't add colours, typefaces, radii or shadows that are not in this file.
- Don't invent components. This design system deliberately has none of these: gallery with a lightbox, map, audio player for own files, slider or carousel, marquee and bento grid.
- Don't slant or embolden a typeface artificially.
- Don't load fonts or embedded content from third parties without consent: an Embed waits for the visitor's consent.
