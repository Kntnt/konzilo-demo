## In short

:::aside{anchor="in-short"}

- We keep what you write in a form for 90 days, and use it only to answer you.
- We do not count your visits, and the web server keeps no log of them.
- Nothing from YouTube loads until you allow it.
- We sell nothing about you, and we do not profile you.

## When you write to us or book a site assessment

The contact form and the booking form send what you fill in to our own form service, on the same server as the site. It keeps your name, company, e-mail address, telephone number and message, with the time you sent them and the language of the page. It does not keep your IP address or the address of the page you sent the form from.

To keep floods of spam out, the form service counts how many forms come from one connection in ten minutes. It counts them in its memory alone, by a code made from your IP address with a secret key, and forgets the count when the ten minutes are up.

Each message is mailed to the people at Ampfield who answer that kind of question, and the form service deletes it after 90 days. A message that looks like spam is deleted after 7 days. When a conversation leads to an offer or an agreement, we copy what we need into our customer system and keep it for as long as we work with your company, and for two years after our last contact.

We do this on our legitimate interest in answering you and in following up a site assessment you asked for (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and, where you ask for an offer, to take the steps you asked for before an agreement (Article 6(1)(b)).

## The newsletter

When you sign up, our form service keeps your e-mail address and the time you gave your consent for 30 days, while we add them to our mailing list. We use them only to send the newsletter, once a month. Every issue has a link to unsubscribe; when you use it, we delete your address from the list within a week. We do this on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

## <a id="video"></a>The video from YouTube

One article, [Can your roof carry solar panels?](/insights/can-your-roof-carry-solar-panels/), shows a video from YouTube. Until you allow YouTube, the page loads nothing from it: a question and a button stand in the video's place.

When you press *Allow and play*, or allow YouTube in the privacy settings, your browser loads YouTube's player. From then on Google Ireland Limited, which provides YouTube, receives your IP address and data about your visit, which may be processed outside the EU and EEA. YouTube may store data in your browser, and Google may use what it learns under its own privacy policy, for its own advertising too. That is why the privacy settings list YouTube under Marketing. We show the video on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. The [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/) says who provides the service, which addresses your browser then contacts, and what the service stores.

## <a id="your-choice"></a>Your choice

The site asks whether you allow content from other services, and keeps your answer in your browser for 12 months: the categories and services you allowed, when you chose, and a random consent ID. It keeps a refusal too, so that it does not ask on every page. We keep no copy of your choice on our server.

You change or withdraw your choice with *Change privacy settings* or *Withdraw consent* at the bottom of every page. The cookie policy shows the choice your browser holds.

## Searching the site

What you type in the search box goes to the site's own search engine, on the same server, which finds the pages that match. It does not keep what you searched for.

## The web server

The web server that sends you the site's pages sees your IP address, as every web server must to answer, but keeps no log of visits: not of the pages you read, and not of addresses that do not exist. We count no visits, and we keep no statistics of how the site is used.

## Where the site runs

The site, its forms and its search run on a server in Sweden that we rent from a hosting company, which processes data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Our backups are kept in the EU. No data from this site leaves the EU and EEA, except to YouTube once you allow it, as described above.

## Your rights

You have the right to know what we keep about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its use restricted, to object to it, and to receive what you gave us in a form a computer can read. Write to privacy@ampfield.example. We answer within a month, and we may ask you to show that the data is yours. We can find what you sent in a form by your name or e-mail address. We keep nothing about your visits, so there is nothing of them to find.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection.

## Changes to this policy

When we change what we keep, we change this page and its date. If a change touches something you consented to, the site asks for your consent again.
