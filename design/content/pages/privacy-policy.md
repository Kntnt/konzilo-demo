## What this policy says

- We keep what you write in a form for 90 days, and use it only to answer you.
- We count visits without storing anything in your browser, and you can object to it.
- Nothing from YouTube or any other service loads until you allow it.
- We sell nothing about you, and we do not profile you.

:::

## When you write to us or book a site assessment

The contact form and the booking form send what you fill in to our own form service, on the same server as the site. It keeps your name, company, e-mail address, telephone number and message, with the time you sent them and the language of the page. It does not keep your IP address or the address of the page you sent the form from.

Each message is mailed to the people at Ampfield who answer that kind of question, and the form service deletes it after 90 days. A message that looks like spam is deleted after 7 days. When a conversation leads to an offer or an agreement, we copy what we need into our customer system and keep it for as long as we work with your company, and for two years after our last contact.

We do this on our legitimate interest in answering you and in following up a site assessment you asked for (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and, where you ask for an offer, to take the steps you asked for before an agreement (Article 6(1)(b)).

## The newsletter

When you sign up, our form service keeps your e-mail address and the time you gave your consent for 30 days, while we add them to our mailing list. We use them only to send the newsletter, once a month. Every issue has a link to unsubscribe; when you use it, we delete your address from the list within a week. We do this on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

## <a id="visitor-statistics"></a>Visitor statistics

We count visits to the site with Matomo, which we run ourselves on our own server. From your first page we count each visit without storing anything in your browser, and without reading your device's properties, from what your browser sends with every request anyway: the address and title of the page, the page you came from, the kind of browser, operating system and device you use, your language, and the country and region your shortened IP address points to. We shorten your IP address by two bytes before it is kept, and we cannot see who you are. The time of day of each visit is kept in our own time zone. We do this on our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used, so that we can improve it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you allow statistics, Matomo also stores a cookie in your browser, so that it can tell new visitors from returning ones, and reads your screen size, details of your device and how fast the page loaded. You can withdraw that consent at any time. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we do not count your visits at all.

We delete the raw data after 25 months. The reports made from it hold totals alone, and we share them with no one outside Ampfield.

## <a id="object"></a>Your right to object to the counting

You may object to being counted at any time, in the privacy settings, under Statistics. You open them with *Change privacy settings* at the bottom of every page. Your objection is kept in your browser for 12 months, and from then on nothing about your visits is sent to Matomo. Since we cannot see who you are, we can only honour an objection made this way, in each browser you use. If you allow statistics later, or clear what the site has stored in your browser, the objection ends.

## Content from other services

Some pages may show content from another service, such as a video on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from that service until you allow it, for that one time or in the privacy settings. Once you do, your browser contacts the service, which receives your IP address and may store cookies and use what it learns under its own privacy policy. The [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/) lists each such service, what it stores and for how long.

## Your choice about privacy

The site remembers which categories you allowed, and when, in your browser for 12 months. Each time you make a choice, it also sends us a record of it: a random consent ID, the time, the choices you made, and the page and language you made them on, but not your IP address. We keep these records for 13 months, so that we can show what you agreed to if you ask.

## Searching the site

What you type in the search box goes to our own search service, on the same server, which finds the pages that match. It does not keep what you searched for, and it receives nothing from your browser but the words.

## Addresses not found, and security reports

When a visitor asks for an address that does not exist, the server notes the address and the time, and nothing about the visitor, so that we can fix broken links. These notes are kept for 30 days after the address was last asked for.

When your browser stops something that the site's security policy does not allow, it may send us a report. We keep only the page, the rule and what was stopped, without your IP address or browser, for 30 days.

The web server keeps no other log of visits.

## Where the site runs

The site, its forms, its search and its statistics run on a server in Sweden that we rent from a hosting company, which processes data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Our backups are kept in the EU. No data from this site leaves the EU and EEA, except to a service whose content you allow, as described above.

## Your rights

You have the right to know what we keep about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its use restricted, to object to it, and to receive what you gave us in a form a computer can read. Write to privacy@ampfield.example. We answer within a month, and we may ask you to show that the data is yours. Since we keep no IP addresses, we can find what you sent in a form by your name or e-mail address, but not your visits.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection.

## Changes to this policy

When we change what we keep, we change this page and its date. If a change touches something you consented to, the site asks for your consent again.
