## What these buildings have in common

A distribution centre of 30,000 m², a factory of the same size and a retail park of a dozen stores have more in common than their trades suggest. Each has a flat roof that nobody uses, a grid connection that was sized years ago, and a bill in which a few peak hours count for much of the cost.

Each also has someone who must report its energy use and emissions, and someone who has been asked to cut both. We work with the property manager, the operations manager and the finance director together, because a system on the roof touches all three.
