## What matters in a factory

Production comes first. We build on the roof and in the yard while the lines run, and connect each system in one planned stop of two to six hours, at night, at a weekend or during your own shutdown.

The grid bill matters more than its price per kilowatt-hour suggests. In many tariffs a third or more of a factory's grid cost is set by its highest peaks, and those come from a few machines starting at once. Pilot shows which, and a battery covers them.

Some processes must not stop. Cold stores, kilns and curing ovens lose a batch in a power cut. A battery with the switchgear for it keeps them running until the grid is back.
