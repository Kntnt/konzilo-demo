## What we aim for

We want everyone who runs a building or a budget to be able to use this site, whether they see, hear, move or read in the ordinary way or not. We aim to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.2.

## How we know

We test the site ourselves: with the keyboard alone, with the screen readers built into Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, at 200% and 400% zoom, and in the browsers' high-contrast modes. Every colour combination of text on its background is checked for contrast when the design is made.

No independent party has audited the site, so we make no claim that it conforms to the guidelines. When an audit has been done, this page will say so, and what it found.

## What works

- Every page can be used with the keyboard alone, and the focus is always visible.
- Menus, tabs, questions and answers, and the choices on the Solutions page work without JavaScript, as the browser's own elements.
- Text can be enlarged to 400% without content being lost, and pages reflow to one column on narrow screens.
- Every picture that carries information has a text alternative.
- Forms have visible labels, say which fields are required, and explain an error beside the field.
- Nothing moves, blinks or plays by itself.

## What we know falls short

- **Tabs.** A screen reader announces each tab as a button that expands and collapses, not as a tab in a row of tabs, and the arrow keys do not move between tabs. Tab and Enter work.
- **The list of case studies.** When you choose topics above the list, the number of case studies shown is not announced again. The list itself shows only the matching case studies, and a screen reader finds them by reading on.
- **The menu beside the questions and answers** does not mark which part you are reading.
- **The video from YouTube** in one article plays in YouTube's own player, whose accessibility is YouTube's. Before it loads, the question and the button in its place work with the keyboard.

We are working on the first three, and this page will say when they are fixed.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on the site does not work for you, or you need content in another form, write to accessibility@ampfield.example or call +46 31 390 06 00. Tell us which page, and what went wrong. We answer within five working days, and where we cannot fix the problem at once, we give you the content another way.
