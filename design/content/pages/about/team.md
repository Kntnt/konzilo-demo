## Who leads what

:::cards

### Ingrid Sandell

![Portrait of Ingrid Sandell](image:portrait-ingrid-sandell)

::eyebrow[Chief Executive Officer]

Co-founded Ampfield in 2014 after twelve years building wind farms across the Nordic countries.

### Mattias Lönn

![Portrait of Mattias Lönn](image:portrait-mattias-lonn)

::eyebrow[Chief Technology Officer]

Co-founder. Wrote the first version of Pilot, and still reviews every change to how it controls a battery.

### Priya Raman

![Portrait of Priya Raman](image:portrait-priya-raman)

::eyebrow[Head of Engineering and Delivery]

Leads the engineers and installers who design and build every system, from the structural check to the handover.

### Elias Forsberg

![Portrait of Elias Forsberg](image:portrait-elias-forsberg)

::eyebrow[Head of Energy Services]

Runs the operations centre and the technicians who keep 610 sites producing, around the clock.

### Sofia Marklund

![Portrait of Sofia Marklund](image:portrait-sofia-marklund)

::eyebrow[Chief Financial Officer]

Structures our power purchase agreements and the financing behind them.

### Daniel Okafor

![Portrait of Daniel Okafor](image:portrait-daniel-okafor)

::eyebrow[Head of People and Safety]

Responsible for hiring and training, and for the safety of everyone who works at height or near high voltage.

### Joost van der Heiden

![Portrait of Joost van der Heiden](image:portrait-joost-van-der-heiden)

::eyebrow[Country Manager, Netherlands]

Opened the Rotterdam office in 2021, after twenty years in Dutch industrial property.

### Hannah Whitcombe

![Portrait of Hannah Whitcombe](image:portrait-hannah-whitcombe)

::eyebrow[Country Manager, United Kingdom]

Opened the Leeds office in 2022, and leads our work for retail parks and distribution sites across the UK.

### Lena Bergqvist

![Portrait of Lena Bergqvist](image:portrait-lena-bergqvist)

::eyebrow[Energy Market Analyst]

Follows electricity prices, grid tariffs and balancing markets, and turns them into the figures in our offers.
