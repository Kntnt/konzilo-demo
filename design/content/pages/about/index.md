## Why we started

Ingrid Sandell had spent twelve years building wind farms, and Mattias Lönn as many writing control systems for power plants. In 2013 they noticed that the largest flat roofs in western Sweden belonged to warehouses and factories that bought every kilowatt-hour from the grid, and that nobody offered to put panels on them and then answer for what the panels produced.

They founded Ampfield the next year, with two engineers and an installer. The first roof was 180 kWp on a warehouse in Gothenburg's harbour. It still runs, and it is still ours to look after.

What has not changed is the idea: one company that designs the system, builds it with its own people, pays for it if the customer prefers, and runs it for as long as it produces. We have since added batteries, charging and our own software, and offices in Malmö, Rotterdam and Leeds.

## What we hold to

:::cards{markers="v"}

### We size for use, not for the roof

A system sized to what the building uses pays back faster than one that fills the roof. We tell you when a smaller system is the better buy.

### The numbers before the signature

Every offer shows its assumptions: the yield, the prices, the share used on site, and what happens to the result if each is wrong.

### Safety stops the work

Anyone on a site of ours may stop a job they think unsafe, and is thanked for it. Work at height and near high voltage is planned before it starts, every time.

### We answer for what we build

Our own installers build, and our own technicians service. We run most systems for their whole life, so we build them to last it.

## Since 2014

:::list{type="description"}

How we got here

- 2014
  - Ingrid Sandell and Mattias Lönn found Ampfield in Gothenburg. The first roof, 180 kWp on a warehouse, produces power in October.
- 2016
  - The first power purchase agreement, in which we own the system and the customer buys its power. 10 MWp in operation.
- 2017
  - The office in Malmö opens, and with it the first systems in Denmark.
- 2018
  - The first battery, 1 MWh, beside a cold store in Skåne.
- 2019
  - The first charging depot, for 40 delivery vans. 100 sites in operation.
- 2020
  - Ampfield Pilot, our own software, takes over the control of every site.
- 2021
  - Joost van der Heiden opens the office in Rotterdam, for customers in the Netherlands.
- 2022
  - Hannah Whitcombe opens the office in Leeds, for customers in the United Kingdom.
- 2024
  - 300 MWp of solar in operation.
- 2026
  - 610 sites, 420 MWp of solar, 95 MWh of storage and 3,800 charge points, run by 140 people.
