## Why people stay

Half of the people who joined us in our first five years still work here.

:::cards{markers="v"}

### Employed by us

Every installer and technician is employed by Ampfield, not hired through an agency. The people who build a system are the people who answer for it.

### Training, paid and planned

Working at height, first aid, high-voltage safety and the makers' own courses, in working hours. Installers from other trades are trained to our standard in their first six months.

### Home most nights

Most jobs are within three hours of your office. The weeks away are planned a month ahead, and paid as such.

### Safety before speed

Anyone may stop a job they think unsafe, and is thanked for it. Our Head of People and Safety reads every incident report the same day.

## How we hire

Four steps, and about three weeks from your application to an answer.

:::cards{markers="1"}

### You apply

A CV and a few lines on why this job. No cover letter needed, and no test before we have spoken.

### A first call

Thirty minutes with the manager you would work for, about the job and about you.

### A meeting

Half a day at the office or on a site, with the people you would work beside. For installers, part of it is on a roof, with the right equipment.

### An answer

Within a week of the meeting, yes or no, and why. If yes, we ask for two references and make an offer.

:::
