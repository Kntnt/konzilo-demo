## How a project runs

For a typical 1 MWp roof, six to nine months pass from the first visit to the first kilowatt-hour. Most of that time is design and waiting for the grid operator's approval. The building work itself takes six to twelve weeks.

:::cards{markers="1"}

### Site assessment

An engineer looks at the roof, the switchboard and the grid connection, and reads twelve months of your meter data. Free, with no obligation.

### Design and offer

A structural check of the roof, a model of the yield hour by hour, and the application to the grid operator. You get a fixed price, and a fixed power price, to compare.

### Build

Our own installers build while the building stays in use. Connecting the system needs one planned stop of two to six hours, at night or at a weekend.

### Run

Pilot watches every system once a minute. When a fault stops production, a technician is on site within two working days.

## Two ways to pay

The system is the same either way. What differs is who owns it, and who carries the cost and the risk.

:::cards

### Buy the system

You pay for the system when it is built and own it from the first day. Every kilowatt-hour it produces is yours, free.

- Payback in six to nine years, where most of the power is used on site
- Panels that keep 87% of their output after 30 years
- Service from us under a separate agreement, if you want it

### Buy the power

We build, own, insure and run the system on your roof. You pay nothing up front and buy what it produces, at a price per kilowatt-hour fixed for 10 to 20 years.

- A price set below what the same kilowatt-hour costs from the grid today
- Service, repairs and insurance included
- When the term ends: extend it, buy the system, or have it removed
