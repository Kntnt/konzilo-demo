## What you get

A small controller in your switchboard room, connected to the meters and systems you have and to the ones we build. It sends their readings to Pilot once a minute, over its own mobile connection or your network.

In Pilot you see each building's use against its own roof's output, the month's peak so far, and what each system has saved. Every user sees the sites they are responsible for, on a screen or a phone.

We wrote Pilot ourselves in 2020, because no product we could buy controlled a battery, a solar roof and a depot of chargers together. It runs every one of our 610 sites today.

## Why our customers use Pilot

:::cards{markers="v"}

### Waste you can see

Compressors running all weekend, lights on in an empty store, a cold room that cycles too often. Most sites find 3% to 8% of their use in the first three months.

### Peaks that never reach the meter

Pilot forecasts each day's peaks from your own history and the weather, and uses the battery and the chargers to keep below them.

### One report, every site

Energy use, own production and emissions per site and month, in the form your sustainability report needs, ready on the third working day.

### Equipment from any maker

Pilot reads meters, inverters, batteries and chargers through open protocols, so it works with what you already have.

:::accordion{first="open" expand="one"}

### Does it work with the equipment we already have?

Almost always. Pilot reads meters, inverters, batteries and chargers through the open protocols most makers use. Before we offer anything we list your equipment and check each item; the rare one Pilot cannot read we name in the offer.

### Do we have to stop anything to install it?

No. The controller is mounted in your switchboard room in a day, and connecting it to the meters needs no stop in your supply.

### How long until it pays for itself?

Most sites find 3% to 8% of their use in waste in the first three months, which pays for Pilot several times over. With a battery or chargers, Pilot's control is what makes them save at all.

### Who looks after it?

We do. Our operations centre in Gothenburg watches every site's controller, and we update Pilot about every two weeks without anything to install at your end.

### Where is our data kept, and who owns it?

Your data is yours. It is kept on servers in the EU, encrypted, and seen only by your users and by our staff who look after your site. You can download all of it, at any time, as files or through Pilot's interface.

### What happens if we stop using it?

Pilot on its own runs month by month, with three months' notice. When you leave, you download your data and we collect the controller. If Pilot controls a system we own under a power purchase agreement, it stays for the term of that agreement.
