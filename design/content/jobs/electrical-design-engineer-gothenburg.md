Our engineers in Gothenburg and Malmö design every system we build in Sweden and Denmark: about 70 a year, from a 300 kWp roof on a workshop to a 3 MWp roof with a battery on a distribution centre. We are looking for an electrical design engineer to join the twelve engineers in Gothenburg.

## What the work is

- Design the DC and AC sides of solar and battery systems: strings, inverters, cables, protection and earthing
- Draw the single-line diagrams, and work out how each system connects to the customer's main switchboard
- Prepare the documents the network operator needs to approve a connection
- Visit sites, about two days a month, to see switch rooms and roofs for yourself
- Answer our installers' questions during the build, and take part in commissioning

On every project you work with a structural engineer, a project manager and the engineer who made the site assessment, and you see your design built, usually within six months of drawing it.

## What you bring

- A degree in electrical engineering, or the same knowledge from experience
- At least three years of designing low-voltage installations in commercial or industrial buildings
- A working knowledge of the electrical installation standards that apply in Sweden
- Swedish and English, spoken and written
- A driving licence

Experience of solar, batteries or charging helps, but we will teach you what you do not know. We care more that you check your own work and say so when something is unclear.

## What we offer

- A permanent, full-time position
- Up to two days a week working from home
- 30 days of paid holiday, and an occupational pension
- A yearly training budget, and the time to use it
- A design reviewed by a second engineer before it leaves the office, every time
