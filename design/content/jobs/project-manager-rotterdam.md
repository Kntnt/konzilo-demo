Our Rotterdam office builds rooftop solar and batteries for manufacturers, logistics companies and property owners across the Netherlands, among them the 5 MWh battery that keeps Kessel Packaging's plant in Dordrecht inside its grid connection. Sites on congested parts of the Dutch grid are asking for batteries faster than one project manager can build them, and we need a second.

## What the work is

- Take over each project when the contract is signed, plan it, and lead it to handover
- Run six to ten projects at a time, from 300 kWp roofs to batteries of several megawatt-hours
- Lead our own installers and electricians on site, and bring in cranes, ground works and extra roofers where a project needs them
- Keep each site's people safe and its business running while we build
- Deal with the network operator on connection dates and inspections
- Report cost, time and risks to the customer and to us every month

You report to Joost van der Heiden, Country Manager, Netherlands, and work with our design engineers in Gothenburg and our service team in Rotterdam.

## What you bring

- At least five years of managing construction or electrical installation projects
- A record of safe work on sites that stayed in use while you built
- Experience of contracts and suppliers, and of saying no to changes nobody has paid for
- Dutch and English, spoken and written
- A driving licence

## What we offer

- A permanent, full-time position
- A salary of €4,600 to €5,800 a month, depending on experience
- An electric company car, or a bicycle and a public transport card if you prefer
- 27 days of paid holiday, and a pension
- A training budget, and a week a year with the engineering team in Gothenburg

## How to apply

Send your CV and a few lines on why this job, in Dutch or English, to [careers@ampfield.example](mailto:careers@ampfield.example) by 13 December 2027, with Rotterdam in the subject line. We read applications as they come in and may fill the position before then. No cover letter is needed. If we go on, the next steps are a thirty-minute call with Joost van der Heiden and then half a day in Rotterdam and on one of our sites, and you get an answer within a week of that meeting.

Questions about the role go to [rotterdam@ampfield.example](mailto:rotterdam@ampfield.example), and Joost van der Heiden answers them himself.
